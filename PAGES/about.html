About Us

Sisonke Auto Care is a leading automotive service provider dedicated to delivering exceptional quality services to our customers.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, affordable and professional vehicle maintenance services while ensuring customer satisfaction and vehicle safety. Our services include engine diagnostics and repairs, brake repairs and replacement, suspension and steering repairs, oil and filter changes, battery testing and replacement, tyre fitting, balancing and puncture repairs, wheel alignment, auto electrical repairs, and general vehicle maintenance.

Our Vision

To become a trusted automotive service provider in the community by expanding our digital presence through a professional business website, Google Business Profile, online service catalogue, transparent pricing, online appointment booking, search engine optimisation and email marketing.

We also aim to improve customer accessibility through click-to-call contact options, Google Maps directions, mobile mechanic services, SMS or email reminders, and clear contact information.
